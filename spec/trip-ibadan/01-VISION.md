# 01 — Vision

## One sentence

**I took a real road trip from Lagos to Ibadan.**

## What this is

**Trip_ibadan** is a browser driving game built around a real Lagos → Ibadan journey. The player chooses a realistic Nigerian intercity vehicle, receives a destination in Ibadan, follows a real mapped road network, handles traffic and road events, and completes the trip.

This is a **destination-based driving game**, not an endless road game. The journey has a start, a route, and a destination.

## Core fantasy

The player should feel like they are making an actual trip from Lagos to Ibadan:

- Choose a vehicle suitable for an intercity trip.
- Start from a Lagos terminal / pickup point.
- Follow real road geometry toward Ibadan.
- See recognisable places and route names.
- Manage speed, traffic, fuel, vehicle condition, and passengers.
- Reach the selected destination in Ibadan.
- Receive a trip result and rewards.

## Vehicle fantasy

The hero vehicles are realistic Nigerian intercity transport vehicles, not Lagos danfos.

Initial vehicle choices may include:

- **Toyota Sienna** — small passenger vehicle / family-style intercity trip.
- **Intercity minibus** — a realistic commercial passenger bus.

Future vehicles can include other common Nigerian intercity buses if they fit the game and licensing/asset constraints.

Vehicle colours should look like ordinary intercity transport or private passenger vehicles. **Do not use the classic yellow-and-black danfo appearance.**

## Why it can grow

The route-based structure allows the game to expand without becoming an open-world project:

- More Lagos starting points.
- More Ibadan destinations.
- More vehicle types.
- Day, evening, night, and rain.
- Traffic density and road incidents.
- Fuel and vehicle maintenance.
- Passenger satisfaction.
- Trip history and leaderboards.
- New Nigerian intercity routes later.

## Product stance

- Fun first.
- Free to play on the web.
- Mobile-first performance.
- Real-world geography where practical.
- Designed around short, repeatable trips.
- Build the route system so additional Nigerian journeys can be added later.

## Success for v1

A player opens the game on a phone, chooses a realistic intercity vehicle, starts in Lagos, sees a real mapped route toward Ibadan, drives through recognisable locations, and successfully reaches an Ibadan destination.

The result screen should clearly communicate:

> **Trip completed: Lagos → Ibadan**

## Full fantasy

Garage in Lagos → choose vehicle → select destination in Ibadan → follow the real route → deal with traffic and road conditions → arrive safely → receive a trip score and earnings.

## What this is not

- Not an endless runner.
- Not an endless fake road with points as the only goal.
- Not a Lagos danfo simulator.
- Not GTA Lagos.
- Not a full open-world recreation of Lagos and Ibadan.
- Not a photogrammetry digital twin of the entire corridor.
- Not a cinematic project that never becomes playable.

## Design principle

**The destination matters more than the distance travelled.**
