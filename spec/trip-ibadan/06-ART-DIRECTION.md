# 06 — Art Direction

## Pillars

1. **The vehicle is the hero.**
2. **The route is real.**
3. **The destination is always clear.**
4. **The world is readable on a phone.**
5. **The Nigerian road-trip identity comes from details, not stereotypes.**

## Visual target

A clean, believable 3D travel-driving game with realistic road geometry, simplified but convincing terrain, and vehicles that resemble transport actually used for Lagos–Ibadan trips.

Do not make the world look like a generic endless racing game.

## Vehicles

### Toyota Sienna

- Realistic proportions.
- Suitable passenger/intercity appearance.
- Neutral or believable private/commercial colour schemes.
- No danfo yellow/black treatment.

### Intercity bus

- Modern commercial minibus / passenger bus silhouette.
- White, cream, silver, blue, green, or other believable operator colours.
- Fictional branding unless licensed branding is available.

The vehicle should look like something a player could realistically encounter on an intercity trip.

## Environment

- Real mapped road geometry.
- Terrain follows the route.
- Vegetation appropriate to the Lagos–Ibadan corridor.
- Simplified roadside buildings.
- Real place names and selected landmarks.

## UI

- Strong contrast over terrain.
- Large touch targets.
- Minimal HUD while driving.
- Destination should remain visually obvious.
- Trip result should be highly shareable.

## Motion

Vehicles should have believable weight and acceleration.

The road should feel like a real road rather than a conveyor belt.

## Audio

The horn, engine, tyre noise, traffic and arrival feedback should establish the Nigerian road-trip identity.
